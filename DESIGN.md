## Brand & Style

This design system translates the heritage, permanence, and tactile prestige of bespoke wedding stationery into an interactive, high-fidelity digital artifact. Built for intimate celebrations, live visual projections, and enduring commemorative archives, it merges traditional bookmaking crafts—letterpress indentation, micro-embossing, and metallic foil stamping—with fluid, accessible web interactions.

The visual direction draws from high-end editorial print and modern warm minimalism:
- **Paper Tactility:** UI surfaces emulate multi-ply, unbleached cotton rag paper (300+ gsm) with micro-textures, generous borders, and unhurried whitespace.
- **Editorial Typography:** High-contrast serif headlines evoke traditional typographic typesetting and engraved vows, paired with balanced humanist sans-serifs for high-legibility messaging at scale.
- **Subtle Luxuries:** Gold foil accents are applied with restraint, strictly reserved for key milestones, monogram insignia, and active focus boundaries rather than broad fills.
- **Quiet Transitions:** Layer changes, input highlights, and wall refreshes rely on soft cross-fades and ambient depth shifts rather than abrupt mechanical cuts.

## Colors

The palette establishes an emotional connection to heirloom paper, velvet ribbon accents, and refined gilding.

- **Canvas & Surface Tones:**
  - Canvas Base: `#FAF8F5` (Warm Alabaster/Ivory) provides a non-glare, tactile foundation reminiscent of hand-milled cotton paper.
  - Card & Container Surface: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp Warm White) sits gently atop the canvas, creating subtle optical stratification without stark blue-white glare.
  - Surface Alt / Field Fill: `#F4EFEA` (Pressed Linen) applied to inner message fields and recessed list cards.

- **Accent Colors:**
  - Primary Accent: `#5E1924` (Deep Burgundy) serves as the primary visual anchor for high-intent actions, primary buttons, couple typography, and active navigational signals.
  - Secondary Accent: `#C5A059` (Soft Champagne Gold) provides metallic warmth for monogram emblems, interactive highlights, active badges, and ornamental separators.
  - Tertiary / Hairline Border: `#E5D8BE` (Soft Gilding) delivers delicate structural boundaries that mimic thin blind debossing and gold-leaf margins.

- **Text & Foreground:**
  - Dominant Text: `#2C2224` (Charcoal Espresso) provides deep, warm contrast for headline typography, guest signatures, and message bodies.
  - Subdued Text: `#7A6F71` (Dusty Taupe) offers neutral hierarchy for timestamps, input placeholders, metadata, and form helpers.

## Typography

The typography system pairs the regal, editorial cadence of `Playfair Display` with the balanced, modern functionality of `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

- **Editorial Hierarchy:**
  - `display` and `headline-lg` are reserved for the names of the couple, main hero title headers, and oversized presentation counters on projection walls.
  - `signature` uses an italicized or soft-weighted Playfair Display style to replicate handwritten signatures at the bottom of guestbook entries and digital blessings.
  - All uppercase labels (`label-md`, `label-sm`) use extended letter-spacing (0.08em to 0.1em) to emulate luxury product engraving and letterpress title caps.

- **Responsive Reflow:**
  - Headings shift down proportionally on mobile viewports using dedicated mobile tokens to maintain vertical rhythm without accidental word wraps or line crowding.
  - Body text remains grounded on a 4px baseline grid, maintaining generous line heights to preserve the unhurried editorial ambiance.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an editorial grid reminiscent of architectural wedding programs and broadsheet stationery layouts.

- **Grid Architecture:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid bounded by a maximum content container of 1440px. The live message wall uses asymmetrical masonry columns (spanning 4 or 6 columns) to emulate letters scattered effortlessly on a banquet table.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with 2rem margins and 1.5rem gutters. Guest sign forms transition to centered 6-column modular panels.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 1.25rem canvas margins and 1rem gutters. All structural message cards expand full-width with vertical stacking.

- **Spacing Rhythm:**
  - Use `space-xl` (3rem) around major visual section breaks, card clusters, and header arrangements to let the canvas breathe.
  - Use `space-lg` (1.75rem) for the interior padding of primary guestbook entry cards.
  - Use `space-md` (1rem) for input interior paddings and inter-element button groups.
  - Use `space-xs` (0.25rem) and `space-sm` (0.5rem) exclusively for icon-label pairing and sub-label lockups.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is achieved through paper-stack tactile layering, soft ambient debossing, and refined gold hairlines instead of heavy multi-directional shadows.

- **Level 0 (Foundation Canvas):**
  - Background `#FAF8F5`. No shadows. Flat, tactile foundation.

- **Level 1 (Stationery Message Cards & Surface Panels):**
  - Background `#FFFFFF` with a crisp 1px hairline border in `#E5D8BE`.
  - Ambient Shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(44, 34, 36, 0.04), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(44, 34, 36, 0.02)`.
  - Simulates thick cotton stationery resting flat upon an ivory silk tablecloth.

- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards, Dropdowns & Floating Entry Modals):**
  - Ambient Shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(44, 34, 36, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(94, 25, 36, 0.03)`.
  - Subtle vertical lift (`translateY(-2px)`) to mimic picking up a physical keepsake card.

- **Level 3 (Live Wall Spotlight & Fullscreen Focus):**
  - Ambient Shadow: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(44, 34, 36, 0.12), 0 0 0 1px #C5A059`.
  - Applied during live stage projection when an incoming guest message is spotlighted before settling into the wall grid.

- **Debossed State (Inputs & Form Trays):**
  - Inset shadow: `inset 0 1px 2px rgba(44, 34, 36, 0.04)` combined with 1px border in `#E5D8BE` to recreate classic blind-debossed letterpress indentations.

## Shapes

The geometry balances vintage cardstock cutting with contemporary software precision:
- Primary container cards, modal frames, and message cards feature calibrated **12px (`0.75rem`)** corner radii, falling within the `roundedness: 2` scale to honor traditional die-cut paper edges without becoming cartoonish or clinical.
- Interactive controls (input fields, dropdown triggers, and utility chips) carry matching `0.5rem` to `0.75rem` radiuses for visual harmony.
- Circular elements are strictly reserved for wax seal stamps, couple monograms, and audio playback indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button (The Royal Burgundy):** Solid `#5E1924` background with `#FAF8F5` text, 12px border radius, 14px uppercase label with 0.08em tracking. On hover: background deepens to `#46121B` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 14px rgba(94, 25, 36, 0.2)`).
- **Secondary Button (Champagne Foil):** Background transparent, 1px solid `#C5A059` border, text `#2C2224`. On hover: `#FAF8F5` fill with gold border transition and soft gold glow.
- **Tertiary / Ghost Button:** No border or background; text `#7A6F71`. Hover state turns text to `#5E1924` with a gentle underline transition.

### Message & Keepsake Cards
- Built on `#FFFFFF` base with 1px `#E5D8BE` border and 12px corner radius.
- Padding uses `space-lg` (1.75rem).
- Layout: Top monogram or initial badge, central guest message set in `body-md`, timestamp set in `label-sm` muted text, finished by the guest signature in `signature` Playfair Display italics.
- Interactive card states include a subtle 1px border color transition to `#C5A059` on cursor focus.

### Input Fields & Text Areas
- Background: Pressed linen tone (`#F4EFEA`) with an inset deboss shadow (`inset 0 1px 2px rgba(44, 34, 36, 0.04)`).
- Border: 1px solid `#E5D8BE`.
- Focus State: Surface shifts to `#FFFFFF`, border sharpens to `#C5A059`, accompanied by an outer gold ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.15)`).
- Placeholders set in `Plus Jakarta Sans` regular, color `#7A6F71`.

### Chips & Filters
- Compact 8px rounded capsules.
- Default state: Canvas white background, 1px `#E5D8BE` border, `#2C2224` text.
- Active/Selected state: Solid `#5E1924` fill with `#FFFFFF` text and subtle gold border hint.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 20px structural boxes with 4px corner radius (checkbox) or 20px circles (radio).
- Border: 1.5px solid `#C5A059`.
- Checked state: Filled with `#5E1924` featuring a crisp ivory checkmark or central solid pip.

### Live Wall Projection Mode
- Specialized responsive display container with high-contrast serif typography scaling up to 72px for banquet room readability.
- Subtle celebratory gold border animation (1px pulsing gold gradient) on newly arrived live entries before smoothly translating into the passive wall layout.
